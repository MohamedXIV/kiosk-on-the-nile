## Review and decisions

No automated tests were added or run. Ink compilation, Godot import and debug/release exports are build gates; actual browser mouse/keyboard interactions and read-only snapshots are separate acceptance evidence.

One fresh independent read-only whole-increment review found four Important issues: split ingredient stacks losing recognized grant identity; a moved phone host retaining an unrestorable contact pose; emergency classification not reconciled across invoice/receipt/occurrence; and one-way stock validation accepting missing consumed days/keys. The material fix pass preserved immutable grant origins through splits and checked aggregate remaining quantities; anchored contact availability and closed it at item/placement publication; reconciled frozen emergency terms/food/markers; and required exact bidirectional stock usage.

The reviewer’s history/pending-term visibility and silent busy-call feedback observations were regraded Important and addressed in that same pass. There were no deferred minors or second reviewer. Emergency quote presence/sequence, signed invoice deadlines, consumed food provenance and truthful repeat-request refusal were strengthened during acceptance.

Rulings retained from the execution ledger:

1. Keep the managed `work` checkout/caches and isolate by branch; no second checkout or main merge. Cost: less filesystem isolation.
2. Higher-priority no-tests instruction governs the skill's TDD steps. Cost: manual/build coverage is narrower than automated regression coverage.
3. One recurring contract plus immutable versions and separately identified extras bounds the first implementation. Cost: no simultaneous independent recurring baskets with the same supplier yet.
4. Calls use authored existing desk/counter host IDs, not a modern mobile/payment app or new physical NPC. Cost: phone art is still placeholder; contact points are named in the guide/menu.
5. Legitimately exhausted ingredients need not retain nonexistent consumption receipts. Existing split descendants retain their origin and aggregate remaining quantities cannot exceed its grant; full historic ingredient consumption cannot be reconstructed independently.
6. A removed/moved destination may remain signed on restore, then skip safely at due. Cash requires separate local confirmation. Supplier repayment settles all remaining principal in this increment; selective/partial supplier repayment remains later scope.
7. Scheduled unattended invoice/system time is the contractual delivery instant; evaluated_at is processing/wake time. Cash timestamps are actual confirmation. This prevents sleeping from extending future payment deadlines.
8. Explicit consume-held menu access delegates existing care behavior. Cost: one menu entry; no altered food/nourishment contract.
